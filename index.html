🏡 A good home

I put some work into getting these data sets ready, but I’m looking for someone who wants to claim each of them: maintain it, give it regular attention, keep it open and available to the community, and reach out to the researchers and other people who might use it.

  • All of these are data sets that run in an automated way to pull data from a government website. None of these have an existing API that the government hosts, and most involve PDFs or web scraping. By putting these on Hugging Face, someone can now query all of them in one place, and by putting them in a website, a user can filter/find what they’re looking for.
  • Each one comes with a GitHub repo for the code, a Hugging Face repo for the data, and a basic website. I'm hosting the websites for now (but I would like to stop).
  • If you want to take one of these over, you should have an engineer, or someone engineer-like. That is, someone who is going to respond if something breaks and periodically look at how they’re going and fix the code if something is off.
  • In some cases, you need an LLM API key because I’m pulling fields out from unstructured text data. I'm using Claude. The bills are small (see each card). There’s no other spend associated with these.
  • I put together a handoff document for each one that should help your engineer take over. For all of these, you’ll need to sign up for some (free) accounts.

Federal J&A Documents

Why federal contracts skipped competition.

  • Refreshes daily
  • Live sites
  • LLM key
Source
Justification notices posted on SAM.gov.
Size
20,019 notices, 22,160 attachments, 78,253 pages. About 11 GB of original files.
Refresh
Daily, started by a Cloudflare Worker (GitHub's own schedule runs hours late).
LLM cost
About $0.15 a day ($4.50 a month).

What you get

  • Every justification notice posted on SAM.gov since January 2025: agency, office, dates and award number
  • The original files and their full text
  • The matching federal award, where one exists
  • Vendor, dollar value, legal authority and a summary, pulled out by an LLM because the notices have no structured data (experimental)
  • A daily email digest of the most notable new ones

Known problems

  • About 9,000 notices are Special Notices picked by title, not formal justifications
  • Some attachments are only links, or were deleted or never public
  • Text read before 2026-11-06 has some split words and missing apostrophes. A text_engine column marks the reader

Code: Apache 2.0 · Data: CC0

OTA Watch

Federal Other Transaction awards.

  • Refreshes daily
  • Live site
  • LLM key
Source
Every Other Transaction record in FPDS. These deals fall outside the usual acquisition rules and aren't in USAspending.
Size
59,581 records, signed 2000-11-15 to 2026-11-04.
Refresh
Daily. The site lists new awards weekly.
LLM cost
About $1 a month.

What you get

  • Every Other Transaction record in the federal contracting system, back to 2000
  • Agency, vendor, dates and dollars obligated for each
  • A flag for deals run through a consortium (fiscal 2022 on)
  • A by-year summary

Known issues

  • Only Defense, Homeland Security and Interior contracting offices appear
  • Use obligated for dollars. FPDS's other value fields disagree with each other
  • The vendor is often a consortium, not the company doing the work

DoD Daily Contracts

Defense contract awards, one row each.

  • Refreshes daily
  • Live site
Source
The Defense Department's daily contract announcements on war.gov.
Size
43,612 awards, 2014-07-01 to 2026-11-05, in 3 files.
Refresh
Daily, about 4–5 pm ET. A broken scrape fails the run, and CI runs the tests.

What you get

  • Every contract award the Defense Department has announced since July 2014
  • The date, military branch and the full award text for each
  • The company and its city and state, where they could be read

Known problems

  • Dollar amounts and contract numbers are only in the text
  • Company and place are pulled from the award text by pattern matching (about 95% of the time) and are blank when unsure

EEOC Appellate Decisions

Federal-sector appeal decisions, with model-read case fields.

  • Refreshes daily
  • Live site
  • LLM key
Source
Every file on EEOC's federal-sector appellate decisions page.
Size
115,882 listed files, about 5 GB. A model read the 5,744 decisions posted since 2025.
Refresh
Daily. A monthly job re-crawls the listing to check it still matches the site.
LLM cost
About $0.003 per new decision, so cents a day.

What you get

  • Every federal-sector appeal decision EEOC posts, with the original files
  • For decisions since 2025: the agency, who appealed, which bases were claimed (race, sex, disability, age, reprisal), whether discrimination was found and whether relief was granted. An LLM extracts these because EEOC publishes no structured data
  • Accuracy figures for each of those fields

Known problems

  • A file number doesn't reliably identify a decision
  • The accuracy check uses an answer key made by Claude agents, not lawyers
  • Posting date is not decision date

Code: MIT · Data: public records

OPM Union Contracts

Every federal union contract, as PDFs and text.

  • Weekly listing check
  • Rest by hand
  • Live site
Source
Every federal union contract in OPM's public database.
Size
1,248 records, 1,243 PDFs. As of 2026-11-01.
Refresh
A weekly job snapshots OPM's listing and opens an issue. Downloading PDFs, extracting text, rebuilding the CSV and flags, and uploading are manual.

What you get

  • Every federal union contract in OPM's database, as PDFs with their text
  • The agency, union and dates OPM lists for each
  • A first-pass flag for contracts terminated under the 2025 executive orders, with sources

Known problems

  • Flags are leads to check, not findings. Court status goes stale fast
  • Scanned agreements have little or no text. No OCR was run

HHS Appeals Board Decisions

HHS Appeals Board decisions back to 1974.

  • Refreshes daily
  • Live site
Source
Every decision the HHS Departmental Appeals Board publishes, via the Internet Archive and a live browser (hhs.gov blocks bots).
Size
9,853 decisions (3,319 Appellate from 1974, 6,534 ALJ from 1985), through 2026-11-02. Plus 224 selected Medicare Appeals Council decisions.
Refresh
Daily. The job fails if the list of published decisions shrinks or comes back empty.

What you get

  • Every decision the HHS Departmental Appeals Board publishes: appellate decisions back to 1974 and judge decisions back to 1985
  • Case details from each decision's header, and the full text
  • A selection of Medicare Appeals Council decisions

Known problems

  • The Council file is a selection the Board chose to post, not everything it decided
  • Category labels are a heuristic with no measured precision or recall
  • Some old scans extract as garbled text without being flagged

The original corpus was built by KMisener90 (this repo is a fork, MIT). The live site and dataset are mine.

Code: MIT · Data: US government works

DC Procurement

DC procurement records, kept after the portal drops them.

  • Refreshes weekly
  • Live site
Source
DC's Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal: contracts, purchase orders, payments and solicitations.
Size
7,544 contract periods, 243,611 purchase orders, 885,916 payment lines, 16,274 solicitations. Pulled 2026-11-02.
Refresh
Weekly, Mondays. Each pull is merged into the archive, so rows the portal drops stay, marked as delisted.

What you get

  • DC's contracts, purchase orders and payments, with solicitations and their documents
  • Weekly history, so records the portal later drops are still here

Known problems

  • A contract row is an option period, not a contract. Summing amounts double counts
  • About half of payments carry no contract number
  • The portal's own terms weren't found. The data.gov entry lists CC BY 4.0

SF-133 Budget Execution

Budget execution for every federal account, by month.

  • Refresh by hand
  • Live site
Source
The 29 agency workbooks OMB posts each year on MAX.gov (the SF-133).
Size
3.06 million rows, FY2018 to FY2026, 7,953 to 9,319 accounts a year.
Refresh
By hand. Download the new year's workbooks, run the build scripts, publish. OMB updates them monthly during the year.

What you get

  • Budget execution for every federal account, month by month, FY2018 to FY2026
  • Total budgetary resources, unobligated balances, obligations and outlays for each account
  • A flag for gift and donation accounts

Known problems

  • Amounts are fiscal-year-to-date. Line 2500 is total budgetary resources, not budget authority
  • Totals here include financing accounts and accounts with no line 2490. The apportionment site leaves some out
  • No October anywhere. FY2019 has no January or February in any workbook
  • The gift-account flag is a title match, not a judgment

Code: MIT · Data: US government works

USAJOBS Questionnaires

The questions on USAJOBS application questionnaires.

  • Refresh by hand
  • Live site
Source
The USA Staffing and Monster questionnaire pages that USAJOBS announcements link to.
Size
52,227 questionnaires and 949,370 questions from the 2025 collection.
Refresh
By hand for now. The fetcher is built and tested but not scheduled. About 251,000 more from late 2025 and 2026 would take about 84 days at a polite rate.

What you get

  • Every questionnaire attached to a USAJOBS announcement since 2025, with the agency and occupation
  • The questions in order, with their answer choices. These are blank questionnaires, with no applicant answers
  • Email addresses are removed from the text (phone numbers are not)
  • The 2.3 million questionnaire ids still to collect

Known problems

  • Many questionnaires share most of their questions, so questions are not unique
  • Monster pages are raw text only. Agency-run portals are not covered
  • I did not find the portals' terms. Check them before reuse

Code: MIT · Text: check the source terms
